WELCOME TO THE PACIFIC

A small island.
A world to explore.

Taniti covers less than 500 square miles, with sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, lush rainforests, and a small active volcano. About 20,000 people call the island home. Fishing and agriculture shaped its economy before the recent growth in tourism.
