DISCOVER TANITI / VISITOR FAQ

A few things to know.

Practical information to help you arrive prepared.

Currency and payments

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are widely accepted. Several banks provide currency exchange.

Electricity

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Health and safety

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees. Violent crime is very rare, but reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes are increasing with tourism.

Alcohol rules

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m. The island brief notes that the drinking age is not strictly enforced.

Holidays and closures

Taniti observes many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may close on holidays, so plan accordingly.
