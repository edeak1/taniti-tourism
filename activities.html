DISCOVER TANITI / ACTIVITIES

Make room for adventure.

Explore the coast, discover the rainforest, or spend an afternoon in town.

Beaches and sightseeing

Explore the white sandy beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay and the native architecture of Taniti City.

Planning details

Boat and bus tours offer ways to see the island. Ask the tour provider about arrangements and availability before your visit.

Outdoor adventures

Hike in the rainforest, visit the active volcano, snorkel, zip-line, or take a chartered fishing tour.

Planning details

Helicopter rides are another way to explore. Contact an activity provider to arrange participation and confirm availability. The supplied island information does not include provider contact details.

Entertainment

Visit the local history museum, art galleries, an arcade, bowling, a movie theater, pubs, a microbrewery, or a dance club.

Planning details

Many activities are in Merriton Landing, on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. A nine-hole golf course is planned for next year; it is not described as currently open.

Plan around national holidays: attractions and restaurants may close. Read visitor information.
